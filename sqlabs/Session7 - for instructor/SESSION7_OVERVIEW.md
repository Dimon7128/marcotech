# Session 7 — Parts, objectives and main goals

Each part opens with its name, objective and three main goals in the presentation. The schedule remains **180 minutes**, including **95 minutes of hands-on work**.

## Part 1 — What should we monitor?

**Slides:** 1–8 · **Time:** 25 minutes · **Hands-on:** 10 minutes

**Objective:** Distinguish service health from prediction quality.

**Main goals:**

1. Identify service signals.
2. Check predictions against labels.
3. Use summaries to protect privacy.

## Part 2 — Drift in SMS messages

**Slides:** 9–19 · **Time:** 35 minutes · **Hands-on:** 15 minutes

**Objective:** Explain changing SMS patterns and their limits.

**Main goals:**

1. Compare reference and current batches.
2. Distinguish data and concept drift.
3. Measure length and URL-like patterns.

## Part 3 — A simple monitoring report

**Slides:** 20–28 · **Time:** 35 minutes · **Hands-on:** 25 minutes

**Objective:** Generate and interpret a simple monitoring report.

**Main goals:**

1. Prepare two monitoring features.
2. Run Evidently and explain one chart.
3. State what remains unknown.

## Part 4 — What happens after an alert?

**Slides:** 29–37 · **Time:** 30 minutes · **Hands-on:** 0 minutes

**Objective:** Choose a response to a monitoring alert.

**Main goals:**

1. Inspect data and processing.
2. Assess available quality evidence.
3. Justify monitoring, investigation or retraining.

## Part 5 — Guided lab and recap

**Slides:** 38–45 · **Time:** 55 minutes · **Hands-on:** 45 minutes

**Objective:** Investigate an SMS incident and justify an action.

**Main goals:**

1. Compare batches and interpret drift.
2. Separate observations from unknowns.
3. Submit a report and decision.
